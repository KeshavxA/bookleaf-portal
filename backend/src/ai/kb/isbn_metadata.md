## ISBN Policy (BookLeaf Knowledge Base)
- Every book published through BookLeaf receives a unique ISBN assigned by BookLeaf.
- ISBNs are registered under BookLeaf's publisher imprint. If an author wants an ISBN under their own imprint, they need to obtain it independently.
- If an author reports an ISBN error (duplicate, wrong book linked), it is treated as a high-priority issue and escalated to the production team.
- Metadata updates (e.g. book description) can be submitted through the dashboard or by emailing the BookLeaf team; changes typically reflect on platforms within 3-5 business days.

## Sample query and how BookLeaf answers it
Sample query: "My book is showing a different ISBN on Amazon than what's on the physical copy."
How to answer: Treat as high priority. Acknowledge this is a serious data issue and that it is BookLeaf's responsibility. Confirm you are escalating to the production team immediately. Provide a 48-hour resolution timeline.
