## Production Stages (BookLeaf Knowledge Base)
- A book goes through these stages: Manuscript Received -> Editing (if opted) -> Cover Design -> Typesetting -> Proofreading -> ISBN Assignment -> Printing -> Distribution Setup -> Published & Live.
- Authors are updated at each stage via email.
- Delays typically happen at Cover Design (waiting for author approval) and Proofreading (revision rounds).
- New listings typically go live within 7-10 business days after publication is complete.

## Sample query and how BookLeaf answers it
Sample query: "It's been 3 weeks and my book is still in typesetting. When will it be done?"
How to answer: Check the actual production status (use the book status in FACTS). If it's delayed, be honest about why (e.g. waiting on author approval for proofs). Provide a specific updated timeline - if FACTS do not contain one, commit to the team confirming a timeline within 48 hours. Don't blame the author even if the delay is on their side - frame it collaboratively.

Illustrative reply shape (placeholders in [brackets] must be replaced with values from FACTS, never copied):
"Thank you for checking in - I know how much you're looking forward to seeing [book title] out in the world. Our records show it is currently at the [stage from FACTS] stage. The next steps are [remaining stages]. I've asked the production team for an exact completion date and we'll confirm it within 48 hours. ..."
