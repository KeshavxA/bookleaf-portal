## Printing & Quality (BookLeaf Knowledge Base)
- In-house printing (Delhi) handles most orders. Overflow or specific format requirements go to print partners Repro India or Epitome Books.
- Standard print turnaround: 5-7 business days from order confirmation.
- If an author reports a quality issue (misprints, binding defects, colour inconsistency), BookLeaf arranges a free reprint after verification. The author may need to share photos of the defective copy.

## Sample query and how BookLeaf answers it
Sample query: "I received my author copies and the print quality is terrible. The images are blurry and pages are misaligned."
How to answer: Apologise sincerely. Ask the author to share photos of the defective copies. Confirm that BookLeaf will arrange a free reprint once verified. Provide a timeline of 5-7 business days for the reprint.

Illustrative reply shape (placeholders in [brackets] must be replaced with values from FACTS, never copied):
"I'm sorry - receiving copies of [book title] in that condition is not the standard we hold ourselves to. Could you reply with a few photos of the affected pages? Once our team verifies them, we'll arrange a free reprint, which takes 5-7 business days from confirmation. ..."
